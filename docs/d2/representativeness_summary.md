# Representativeness and bias: summary

Generated by `bufferengine.pipeline.d2_evidence`. Every number below is read from a table in `docs/d2/tables/` (file named in brackets). Figures are in `docs/d2/figures/`.

## Findings

1. **AA is a minority of US reporting-carrier traffic.** AA mainline is 14.0% of all reporting-carrier flights on average (monthly range 13.7% to 14.5%), computed from 11,730,351 raw flights; it is not a sample of the whole industry. AA's late-arrival rate (25.9% of operated flights) differs from all carriers' (22.8%). [aa_share_by_month.csv]
2. **Flights are concentrated in a few airports.** The top 10 origins carry 61.0% of flights and the top 25 carry 77.1%; 42 of 137 origin airports have under 0.1% of flights each and together 1.6%. Their rates differ: late-arrival 28.4% at the top 10, 22.1% at airports 11-30 and 21.7% elsewhere; cancellation 2.15%, 2.15% and 2.12%. Results will mostly describe hub behaviour. [origin_airport_share.csv, origin_airport_groups.csv]
3. **Night and very early hours are thin.** Departures scheduled 00:00-04:59 are 0.33% of flights and 22:00-23:59 are 4.37%. Hourly late-arrival rates range from 10.1% to 37.4%, so hours with few flights have noisy rates. [by_hour.csv]
4. **Domestic scheduled service only; no foreign airports found.** Of the airports matched in OurAirports, flights touching a foreign country: 0 flight ends; flights touching US territories (Puerto Rico, US Virgin Islands and similar): 26,456 flight ends. Airports not matched by IATA code: PBI. AA's international flying is therefore **absent**, although the D1 charter describes AA as domestic and international. [country_check.csv, non_50_state_airports.csv, state_codes.csv]
5. **The calendar is short.** The table covers 20 months. Calendar months absent from the test window: Sep, Oct, Nov, Dec (BTS had published only to August 2026). Absent from the training window: Oct, Nov, Dec. Calendar months with data in only one year: Sep, Oct, Nov, Dec. Each calendar month appears once or twice, so seasonal and year effects are confounded. [split_coverage_by_month_of_year.csv]
6. **One severe storm sits in the test window.** The five storm days (2026-01-24 to 2026-01-28) had 50.1% of flights cancelled against 1.8% on all other days; late-arrival rate 55.9% against 26.0%; only 30.6% of flights were in usable chains against 86.3%. They are about 1.8% of test-window flights, so they move test averages disproportionately. Other January days: cancelled 1.3%. [storm_vs_normal.csv]
7. **Disrupted days under-contribute to usable chains.** Usable-chain share by month runs from 71.3% to 83.6% of tail-days (lowest 2026-01); the optimiser sees fewer chains exactly when disruption is highest. [usable_by_month.csv]
8. **Tail swaps are uneven.** Swap-suspect pairs per 100 candidate pairs: 4.52 at the top-10 airports, 0.72 at airports 11-30, 0.16 elsewhere; by month from 1.62 to 5.14 (highest 2026-07). Usable tail-days by base airport: top 10 80.4%, 11-30 80.6%, other 80.0%. [swaps_by_month.csv, swaps_by_airport_group.csv, usable_by_airport_group.csv]

## Who or what is under-represented

- **Other carriers and the rest of the industry**: AA is 14.0% of reporting-carrier flights; low-cost, regional and cargo operations are not covered. [aa_share_by_month.csv]
- **Small airports**: 42 airports with under 0.1% of flights each, 1.6% of flights combined. [origin_airport_share.csv]
- **Overnight and early-morning flying** (00:00-04:59): 0.33% of flights. [by_hour.csv]
- **International flying**: none in the table; foreign flight ends = 0. [country_check.csv]
- **September to December in the test window**, and any period after August 2026: absent. Test months present: Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, May, Jun, Jul, Aug. [split_coverage_by_month_of_year.csv]
- **Autumn and winter in the training window**: training has Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep of 2025 only; October to December 2025 is validation only. [split_coverage_by_month_of_year.csv]
- **Severe disruption**: one storm episode (5 days) in the whole table. [storm_vs_normal.csv]
- **Days with broken chains**: months with the lowest usable-chain share (71.3% of tail-days). [usable_by_month.csv]

## Not verified

- Reasons for AA's differences from the industry are not tested here. The BTS file does not identify regional (American Eagle) flying, which is reported by other carriers.
- The country check uses OurAirports country codes matched on IATA code; an airport whose IATA code changed or is not in OurAirports appears under 'not matched'.
